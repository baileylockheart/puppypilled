I genuinely am shocked to be back, but it's happened!! I've been away for an insanely long time, but I'm re-puppified, trust 🙏

I've since colored his ears a bit, restuffed him to make his legs firm and stomach soft, and I even added some poly pellets and aquarium rocks to make him weighted! He's now 2lbs, which isn't a LOT, but makes him just comfy enough to be nice to cuddle and easy to carry.

I've grown so interested in ESSAs that I genuinely think this might be a new hyperfixation for me. I've carefully fixed Daengmo's fur, lifting the fur that was trapped in his seams to make him more evenly soft.
